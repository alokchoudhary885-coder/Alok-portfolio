## Brand & Style — Minimalist Monochrome Edition

The design system projects high-caliber technical authority, architectural precision, and bleeding-edge digital craftsmanship. Designed specifically for an elite full-stack developer portfolio, it balances deep-space minimalism with high-contrast pure white (#FFFFFF) accents over a pitch-black void (#000000).

The aesthetic fuses **Technical Brutalism** with **Dark Glassmorphism**:
- Ultra-deep, pure black canvas (#000000) anchored by razor-sharp geometric alignment.
- Laser-focused pure white (#FFFFFF) highlights used strictly for visual hierarchy, kinetic energy, and interaction feedback.
- Tactile, frosted dark acrylic overlays that reveal structural depth without cluttering the canvas.
- Precision engineering cues: micro-badges, tabular data layouts, monospaced metadata, and controlled monochromatic glows.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around severe contrast, absolute darks, and high-frequency luminescent highlights:

- **Primary (`#ffffff`)**: Pure Radiant White. Used for high-priority CTAs, active status indicators, key headlines, kinetic focal points, and glow boundaries.
- **Secondary (`#f0f0f0`)**: Cool White. Used for hover states, gradient stops, secondary metrics, and refined accent borders.
- **Tertiary (`#161616`)**: Deep Charcoal. Subtle container fills, selection backgrounds, and low-contrast surface differentiation.
- **Neutral Base (`#000000`)**: The Deep Void. The dominant canvas background, ensuring maximum luminous pop for white accents and complete OLED black immersion.
- **Surface Scale**:
  - `surface-glass`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` with backdrop filtering.
  - `surface-hover`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
  - `surface-border`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.
  - `surface-border-subtle`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`.
- **Text & Foreground**:
  - `text-primary`: `#ffffff` (High-contrast pure white).
  - `text-secondary`: `#a0a0a0` (Muted technical gray).
  - `text-accent`: `#ffffff` (Electric white for inline anchors and badges).
  - `text-dim`: `#4a4a4a` (Metadata, code comments, and layout guides).
